<momo_signature_spec>
**Build (create) sign data** (D-10): concat key=value với `&` separator, KHÔNG sort, theo đúng thứ tự MoMo doc:
```
accessKey={accessKey}&amount={amount}&extraData={extraData}&ipnUrl={ipnUrl}&orderId={orderId}&orderInfo={orderInfo}&partnerCode={partnerCode}&redirectUrl={redirectUrl}&requestId={requestId}&requestType={requestType}
```

**IPN sign data** (verify — lexicographic key order theo MoMo IPN doc):
```
accessKey={accessKey}&amount={amount}&extraData={extraData}&message={message}&orderId={orderId}&orderInfo={orderInfo}&orderType={orderType}&partnerCode={partnerCode}&payType={payType}&requestId={requestId}&responseTime={responseTime}&resultCode={resultCode}&transId={transId}
```

**Algorithm:** HMAC SHA256, hex encoded (lowercase). Java: `Mac.getInstance("HmacSHA256")`.

**Amount:** raw VND (KHÔNG nhân 100 như VNPay).

**Identity mapping:**
- MoMo `requestId` = our `paymentSessionId` (idempotency key per request)
- MoMo `orderId` = our `paymentSessionId` (MoMo requires unique per request)
- MoMo `extraData` = Base64(JSON.stringify({orderId: <our order id>})) để link back khi IPN về
- MoMo `transId` (số nguyên long) = `paymentTransactionNo` lưu DB
</momo_signature_spec>
